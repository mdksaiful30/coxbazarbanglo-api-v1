COXBAZARBANGLO

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026 · Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. About this policy

Coxbazarbanglo is a rest-house reservation and management service for authorized administrators and staff. This policy covers its mobile applications and website, including information about staff accounts and guests entered by authorized users. The application is developed by Zaman’s Dev.

For privacy questions, corrections, or account and data-deletion requests, contact info@learnwithzaman.com.

2. Information the service handles

  • Staff accounts: name, email address, role, account status, password authentication information, and sign-in sessions.
  • Guest details: name, phone number, and, when supplied, email address, address, designation, and organization.
  • Bookings: stay dates, room allocations, guest counts and categories, reference letters and dates, booking status, and related notes or requests.
  • Financial records: recorded charges, payments, payment methods and references, balances, refunds, invoices, and credit notes.
  • Documents: reference files selected for upload, such as PDF or image documents, and booking confirmations generated or shared through the service.
  • Service activity: changes to records, approval history, account/device session labels, authentication-token activity, notification destinations and delivery status, and language and appearance preferences. Web hosting and security logs may also record IP addresses, browser information, request times, and errors.

Guest information can be provided by authorized staff rather than directly by the guest. Staff should enter and share information only when authorized to do so.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate staff, control access, manage reservations and room availability, support guest stays, record payments and refunds, prepare reports and confirmations, send requested operational messages, maintain an audit history, and investigate service errors or unauthorized activity. Preferences are used to remember the selected language and theme.

4. Sharing and service providers

  • Authorized rest-house personnel can access records according to their assigned permissions.
  • Hosting, storage, and configured email or messaging providers process information needed to operate the service. If SMS or WhatsApp delivery through Twilio is enabled, the destination and message content are provided to that provider.
  • When a user downloads a confirmation or selects a device sharing, email, or WhatsApp action, the chosen content is sent to the selected recipient or application. That application’s own privacy practices apply to its handling of the content.
  • Information may be disclosed when necessary to comply with applicable legal requirements or to investigate misuse and protect the service.

The application does not include advertising or advertising-tracking SDKs. Personal information is not sold or used for targeted advertising.

5. Device storage and file access

The mobile app stores sign-in tokens using platform secure storage and remembers preferences on the device. The website uses session cookies and browser storage for authentication and preferences. The app accesses files selected by the user through the device file picker and saves or shares files only through the actions the user selects. It does not require access to the device’s address book, microphone, or precise location for its core functions.

Signing out revokes the current mobile session. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser storage removes local app data but does not delete server-side accounts, booking records, or files already downloaded or shared.

6. Security

The production app communicates with the server over HTTPS. Passwords are stored as hashes, access is restricted to authorized accounts, and mobile sessions can be revoked. These safeguards reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep account credentials private and protect downloaded booking documents and shared files.

7. Retention

Staff account information is retained while access is needed for the service. Booking, guest, payment, document, and audit records are retained for the purposes described here and for applicable office recordkeeping, financial reconciliation, security, dispute resolution, and legal obligations. Retention depends on the record and the requirements that apply; this policy does not promise a fixed automatic deletion period. Contact the privacy address to ask what retention requirements apply to a particular record.

Closing an account does not necessarily remove historical booking or financial records. Information that must be retained may remain in restricted records or backups until the applicable retention need ends.

8. Access, correction, and deletion requests

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of information about you, or request closure of a staff account, by emailing info@learnwithzaman.com with the subject “Coxbazarbanglo privacy or deletion request.” You do not need to install or sign in to the app to send a request.

Provide the email address associated with your staff account or enough information to identify your guest booking. Do not send your password, sign-in token, or unnecessary sensitive documents. Identity and authority may need to be verified before a request is processed.

Requests are reviewed with the responsible rest-house personnel. Data that is no longer necessary can be considered for removal or anonymization. If records need to be retained for the purposes explained above, the applicable limits can be explained to the requester. Staff accounts are created by authorized administrators; public self-registration is not offered.

9. Children

This is a workplace management application intended for authorized adult staff, not a service directed at children. Staff should avoid entering unnecessary information about minors. Concerns about a child’s information in a booking record can be raised through the privacy contact.

10. Policy updates and contact

This page may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The last-updated date identifies the current policy.
